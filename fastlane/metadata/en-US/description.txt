LabelFold is a food-label collection for people who want to look up packaged foods, keep notes, compare nutrients and follow selected allergens.

• Search “Open Food Facts” by name, brand or barcode, or use “Scan”.
• Keep labels in a “Collection”, bookmark and filter them, and add a “Personal note”.
• Read an “Ingredient list”, nutrition per 100 g or 100 ml, “Nutri-Score” when provided, declared allergens and possible traces.
• Select allergens in “My allergens” and see matching tags on labels and in “Side by side”.
• Compare two labels’ nutrients and allergen profiles when their nutrition units match.
• Use “Available offline” starter products and previously opened labels.
• View “Bookmarked labels” in a widget.

Open Food Facts is community-maintained. Allergen tags may be incomplete; check the current packaging before purchase.
